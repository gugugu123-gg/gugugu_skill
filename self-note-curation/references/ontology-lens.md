# Ontology Lens

Use this lens to sort the source into six grounded dimensions.

Required dimensions:

- Goal
  - what the text is trying to achieve
- Agent
  - who is acting, deciding, judging, or being affected
- Resources
  - assets, capabilities, information, tools, time, or support already mentioned
- Constraints
  - limits, tradeoffs, blockers, or conditions named in the source
- Process
  - the sequence, mechanism, or way the thing works
- Outcome
  - result, expected result, or feared result already present in the source

Rules:
- Use the source wording where possible.
- Do not force every slot to be filled.
- Do not infer hidden actors, hidden goals, or hidden outcomes.
